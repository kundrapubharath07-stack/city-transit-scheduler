Exam & Viva Preparation Guide

12 Key Defense Questions & Architectural Highlights

1 Problem Statement & Motivation: Solves municipal bus scheduling bottlenecks by combining spatial graph networks with empirical passenger ticketing telemetry.
2 Relational Database (DBMS): Normalized schema (3NF) ensuring UNIQUE(route_id, stop_sequence) integrity and cascading referential constraints.
3 Graph Theory Formalization: Models bus stops as graph vertices $V$ and interconnecting road links as directed multi-edges $E$ in an adjacency list.
4 Algorithmic Traversal (ADSA): Breadth-First Search (BFS) operates in $O(V+E)$ time using a FIFO queue to determine the absolute minimum transit transfers.
5 OOP Modularity: Decoupled entities (Bus, Route, Stop, Schedule) adhere to Single Responsibility Principle (SRP) and provide clean mutators.
6 Dynamic Capacity Algorithm: Automatically triggers extra bus recommendation whenever passenger demand exceeds fleet capacity by $\ge 90\%$.
7 RESTful API Architecture: Clean JSON-based endpoints with token-based authentication header verification and CORS compatibility.
8 Public-Admin Segregation: Strict view isolation protecting administrative mutations while providing rapid, responsive access to passengers.
9 ETM Telemetry Processing: Real-time aggregation of boarding volumes per stop to isolate high-demand bottlenecks across Kakinada.
10 Cryptographic Security: Password hashes generated using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 rounds and unique 16-byte random salts.
11 Haversine Geodesic Math: Great-circle distance calculations between geographic latitude/longitude coordinate pairs.
12 Future AI Expansion: Structured data interfaces ready for integration with random forest and neural network transit demand models.
